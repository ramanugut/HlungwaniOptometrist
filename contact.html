Quick answers

Useful information before you contact the practice.

Can I book online?

Yes. Complete the appointment request form and your details will open in WhatsApp for you to send to the practice.

Is the appointment confirmed immediately?

No. The form sends a request. The practice will confirm the available date and time with you.

What are your opening hours?

Please call or WhatsApp the practice to confirm current opening hours before travelling.

I am not sure which service I need. What should I do?

Choose “Not sure - please advise” on the appointment form or contact the practice directly and explain what you need help with.
