Know the signs

When your eyes are telling you something

Changes in vision, discomfort and recurring eye strain are good reasons to book an eye check. These examples are general information and are not a diagnosis.

Book an Eye CheckCall Us

Common reasons to come in for a check-up

If any of these sound familiar, an eye examination can help identify what needs attention.

Reduced distance vision

Road signs, television or other distant objects may appear less clear than before.

Blurry or double vision

Vision that is consistently blurred or doubled should be assessed rather than ignored.

Difficulty reading small print

If reading fine text has become harder, your near vision may need to be checked.

Headaches and eye strain

Frequent headaches, tired eyes or discomfort during screen use can be linked to visual strain.

Pain, rubbing or tearing

Persistent irritation, excessive tearing or discomfort can be a reason to have your eyes examined.

Itching or discharge

Recurring itching or unusual discharge may need professional assessment, especially when symptoms continue.

Burning eyes

A burning sensation can have several causes, including dryness or irritation, and should be checked if it persists.

Sensitivity to light

If ordinary light suddenly feels unusually uncomfortable, it is worth discussing during an eye check.

Urgent symptoms: sudden vision loss, a serious eye injury, severe eye pain or a sudden major change in vision may need urgent medical attention. Do not wait for a routine appointment if the situation feels urgent.

Long-term eye health

Regular checks can help detect problems earlier

Some eye problems may develop gradually. Screening can help identify signs that need monitoring, more testing or referral.

  • Routine vision changes
  • Eye strain and recurring headaches
  • Screening related to chronic eye conditions
  • Changes noticed between previous eye tests
